# Choir Arranger

Balanced choir lineups, seat plans and sign-off sheets.

**Live:** https://choirarranger.useful-indeed.com

Choir Arranger helps choir leaders and music ministries plan who sings at each performance or worship service. List your singers by voice part and your musicians, set your dates, and it builds balanced lineups, a visual seat plan and a printable sign-off sheet for every one.

## How to use

1. Add your members: name, voice part (soprano, alto, tenor, bass) or instrument.
2. Add your performances or services with date, time and venue.
3. Set the rules — how many per part, rotation, conductor and seating.
4. Review the lineups and seat plans, then download them as PDF, image or CSV, or copy a chat-ready summary.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is stored. Member lists and schedules live only in the open tab and are erased when you close or refresh it.

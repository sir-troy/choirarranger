Questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is my members' information saved anywhere?

No. Names, voice parts and schedules stay in this browser tab only. Refresh or close the page and they're erased. Download your PDF before leaving.

Can I upload my list from Excel?

Yes. In Excel choose File → Save As → CSV, then upload it. Use two columns: name and voice part (or instrument for musicians).

What's in the PDF?

For each performance: a landscape seat plan page, then a portrait sign-off form with name, voice part, seat, an acceptance signature and a performance signature.

Can I use this for weekly worship services?

Yes. Add each service as a performance (for example, every Sunday of the month) and the tool rotates members fairly across them.

Can the same person sing in every performance?

Your choice. Turn on repeats with an optional limit, or turn it off so each person performs once and everyone gets a turn.

Disclaimer · Zero data retention. Choir Arranger runs entirely in your browser. Member names, voice parts, instruments, availability and schedules are held only in this page's memory while it is open. They are not uploaded, logged, saved in cookies, local storage or any database, and not shared with anyone. Files you upload are read on your device and never leave it. Files you download (PDF, image, CSV) are saved only to your own device. When you refresh or close this tab, your choir data is permanently erased and can't be recovered.

Nothing you enter is stored by this site. Lineups and seat plans are suggestions based on the rules you set; please review them before sharing with your group.
